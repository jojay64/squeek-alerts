Wheel running

Fires once when the wheel goes from idle to active (spinning). Being awake is not enough: the wheel has to turn. No repeats while he keeps running.

The hamster is running!The wheel just started spinning (22.4 rpm).

Wheel stopped

Fires after 3 idle checks in a row (about 15 min) once the wheel was spinning, so a short pause is not announced.

The wheel stoppedBlocks wait for the next sprint.

Checkpoint ready

Fires when the contract says a round is settled and the core is not paused. Always check and simulate before you send anything.

Checkpoint is readySqueekMining reports a settled round waiting.

Block milestone

Fires each time the settled block height passes a multiple of 100.

Ways to get them
1

Telegram channel · easiest

Open t.me/squeek_alerts and press Join. Every alert lands in the channel and Telegram pings your phone. No account to create, nothing to configure.

2

GitHub notifications

On the repository click Watch > Custom > Issues. Each alert becomes an issue and GitHub pushes it to your phone.

3

RSS

Add the feed.xml file of the repository to any feed reader.

4

Your own bot or Discord

Fork the repository, add a bot token or a webhook as a secret, done. The secrets stay in your fork and are never printed.
